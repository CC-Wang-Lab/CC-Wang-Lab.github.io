\begin{note}

The objective of this study is to design and develop a novel 3D-IC microchannel water-cooling integration technology, which involves fabricating a multilayer silicon structure containing microchannels and heaters, with water serving as the working fluid. The study aims to simulate and verify the water-cooling performance of this 3D-IC microchannel system. This research targets a heat flux of 2000 W/cm². Currently, under a 60 V input, the system is capable of dissipating a heat flux of 1188 W/cm².
\end{note}
