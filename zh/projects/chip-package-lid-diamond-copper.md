\begin{note}
In this experimental setup, a DC power supply controls the heating rod and heating block to heat the test area. A cooling fan provides forced-convection cooling to the test specimen, while an air duct equipped with a nozzle is used to quantify the airflow rate. An infrared camera mounted directly above the cooling surface monitors the temperature distribution and records data from 30 designated measurement points.
\end{note}
