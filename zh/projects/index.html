研究專案

本實驗室目前進行中的實驗與計算熱工程研究專案。

電子與 AI 散熱 Hybrid Vapor Chamber–Heat Pipe Module for Thermal Management of Gaming Laptops This experimental system evaluates an additively manufactured hybrid vapor chamber–heat pipe module for the thermal management of gaming laptops. Heat is transferred from the heating block to the test specimen and then conducted through the heat pipes to the fins on both sides. Forced-convection cooling is provided by airflow conditioned through a wind tunnel. An infrared thermal camera positioned below the specimen measures the surface temperature distribution to evaluate temperature uniformity and heat transfer performance.
電子與 AI 散熱 Experimental Investigation on Thermal Spreading of Chip Package Lids – Vapor Chamber Lid & PHP Lid This experiment investigates the thermal spreading performance of different chip package lids. The samples are heated from the bottom, while an infrared camera captures their surface temperature distributions. The tested samples include a pure copper plate, a diamond–copper composite, and an additively manufactured copper vapor chamber. For the vapor chamber, various working fluids and filling ratios are evaluated to determine the optimal operating conditions.
電子與 AI 散熱 Experimental Investigation on Thermal Spreading of Chip Package Lids On Diamond-copper composite Lid In this experimental setup, a DC power supply controls the heating rod and heating block to heat the test area. A cooling fan provides forced-convection cooling to the test specimen, while an air duct equipped with a nozzle is used to quantify the airflow rate. An infrared camera mounted directly above the cooling surface monitors the temperature distribution and records data from 30 designated measurement points.
電子與 AI 散熱 Immersion Cooling Microchannel Lid Cold Plate This technology introduces microchannels within the CPU package's IHS, enabling the IHS itself to serve directly as a cold block, thereby eliminating the need for TIM2 interface material.
電子與 AI 散熱 Active Heat Transfer Enhancement in Oil Immersion Cooling Air Bubbling Generator
電子與 AI 散熱 Pulsating jet impingement – Testing trainsent and steady cold plate performance with PG25 and the like fluid Pulsating Jet Impingement Cooling
電子與 AI 散熱 Novel Embedded Microfluidic Interlayer Cooling Solutions for Effective Thermal Management of 3D IC Chip Stack The objective of this study is to design and develop a novel 3D-IC microchannel water-cooling integration technology, which involves fabricating a multilayer silicon structure containing microchannels and heaters, with water serving as the working fluid. The study aims to simulate and verify the water-cooling performance of this 3D-IC microchannel system. This research targets a heat flux of 2000 W/cm². Currently, under a 60 V input, the system is capable of dissipating a heat flux of 1188 W/cm².
沸騰與兩相熱傳 Two-Phase Closed Loop Thermosyphon (TPCT) cooling system This visualized two-phase closed-loop thermosyphon cooling system uses 3M™ HFE-7100 electronic fluorinated dielectric fluid as the working fluid. The system consists of a custom-made glass chamber for flow visualization, a ceramic heater, a copper block, a coil-type heat exchanger, and a cooling-water bath, with the operating pressure maintained below 1 atm. The study investigates vapor–liquid interaction, flow competition, and blockage in the condensate return passage during condensate reflux and vapor generation, as well as their effects on heat transfer performance. Experiments were conducted at filling ratios of 13%, 21%, and 45%; cooling-water temperatures of 10℃, 15℃, and 20℃; and input powers of 50, 100, 150, and 200 W.
沸騰與兩相熱傳 Experiment on Pulsating heat pipe and sintered heat pipe – Especially in Freezing conditions Currently: Behavior of water-methanol mixture on the PHP and heat pipe with pure water and water-methanol mixture under subzero temperature (-20~-40 °C)
沸騰與兩相熱傳 Effects of Working Fluid and Filling Ratio on the Cooling Performance of a Two-Phase Thermosyphon Heat Sink Under Free convection environment This ongoing study investigates the effects of working fluid and filling ratio on the cooling performance of a two-phase thermosyphon heat sink. Heating power, evaporator and condenser temperature distributions, thermal resistance, and parameters related to high-power chip cooling in data centers are evaluated to determine the optimal filling ratio for each fluid and establish recommended filling-ratio ranges.
空調冷凍、熱泵與除濕 The Effect of Pre-Charge Pressure in an Expansion Tank on the Water Supply System This study primarily investigates the impact of expansion tanks on water distribution systems under different pre-charge pressures. By combining fluid temperature, flow rate, pre-charge pressure, and relevant parameters of data center water-cooling systems, the study evaluates the cavitation effects on pumps and proposes a pressure range as a design reference.
空調冷凍、熱泵與除濕 Heat Transfer Performance of a Two-Stage Compression Super-critical CO₂ Chiller This test facility is used to (1) performance evaluation of CO₂ (2) provide a test cold plate system having 15 kW capacity and applicable for multiple cold plate system to examine mal-distribution issues for 2-phase cold plates subject uneven heat loading.
