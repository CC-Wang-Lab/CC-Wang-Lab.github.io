\begin{note}
Pulsating jet impingement cooling combines a **distributed return-nozzle design** with **pulsating inlet-flow control** to improve cooling uniformity and heat-transfer performance. The distributed return flow reduces crossflow interference between adjacent jets, allowing more uniform impingement on the cold plate, while a solenoid valve driven by a function generator periodically modulates the inlet flow rate and velocity. This repeated acceleration and deceleration disrupts the near-wall flow and prevents continuous thermal boundary-layer development, promoting repeated boundary-layer renewal and thereby enhancing local convective heat transfer compared with steady jet operation.
\end{note}
