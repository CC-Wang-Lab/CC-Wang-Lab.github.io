\begin{split}{wind-tunnel-environmental-room}
The LW-4900 AMCA wind tunnel operates under controlled environmental conditions, including temperature, cooling, heating, and humidity. The ambient condition is held between 10 and 30 °C dry bulb and between 15 and 25 °C wet bulb. Airflow is supplied by a compressed-air-assisted fan system and adjusted using selectable nozzles. The airflow rate is determined from the measured pressure drop and covers a range of 40–1,107 CFM. Operating data are captured and recorded using the LW9015M data acquisition program.
\end{split}
