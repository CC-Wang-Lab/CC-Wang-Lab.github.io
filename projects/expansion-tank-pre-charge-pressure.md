\begin{note}
This study primarily investigates the impact of expansion tanks on water distribution systems under different pre-charge pressures. By combining fluid temperature, flow rate, pre-charge pressure, and relevant parameters of data center water-cooling systems, the study evaluates the cavitation effects on pumps and proposes a pressure range as a design reference.
\end{note}
