Research areas

Electronics & AI Cooling Air, liquid, two-phase and immersion thermal management, from chip package to full rack.
Data-Center Thermal Management Airflow management, rack and room-level thermal behavior, cooling energy and waste-heat recovery.
Boiling & Two-Phase Heat Transfer Pool and flow boiling, enhanced and porous surfaces, high-speed visualization, critical heat flux.
Heat Exchangers & Augmentation Fin-and-tube, louver and wavy fin geometries, compact exchangers, pressure drop, fouling forecasting.
HVAC&R, Heat Pumps & Dehumidification High-temperature heat pumps, frost and defrost control, ionic-fluid dehumidification, EV thermal systems.
AI-Assisted Thermal Engineering Physics-informed neural network surrogates, smart thermal control, reinforcement learning, forecasting.

Laboratory head

Chi-Chuan Wang

Chair Professor, Department of Mechanical Engineering

  • Fellow, ASHRAE (2006)
  • Fellow, ASME (2009)
  • Outstanding Research Award, Ministry of Science and Technology (2017)

Professor Chi-Chuan Wang leads thermal-engineering research at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University. His work spans electronics and data-center cooling, boiling and two-phase heat transfer, heat exchangers, HVAC&R, and AI-assisted thermal control.

Before joining National Chiao Tung University as a professor in 2010, he spent twenty-one years in industrial R&D at the Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI), from 1989 to 2010. He was a Visiting Industrial Fellow at the University of California, Berkeley, in 2003 and earned his PhD in Mechanical Engineering from National Chiao Tung University.
