## Biography

Maysam Gholampour is a postdoctoral researcher working on thermal-system modeling, simulation and visualization. His work connects solver outputs with engineering decisions by pairing validated models with real-time visualizations of model behavior.

His current work includes CFD analysis of airflow through electronics-cooling hardware and real-time rendering of flow fields, allowing the effects of design changes to be evaluated visually.

## Background

His background is in mechanical engineering, with a focus on thermal and energy systems.
