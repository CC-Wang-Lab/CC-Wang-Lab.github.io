## Biography

Thermal system modeling, simulation and visualization. Most of my work sits between a solver and a
decision: a model is only useful if somebody can see what it says and act on it, so the same
project usually produces both a validated model and a way to look at it while it runs.

Current work in the lab covers CFD of air paths through electronics cooling hardware, and
real-time rendering of those flow fields so that the effect of a design change can be watched
rather than tabulated.

## Background

Mechanical engineering, with a focus on thermal and energy systems.
