Facilities

Experimental rigs, environmental chambers, instruments and computational resources used by the laboratory.

Boiling & Two-Phase Heat Transfer Two-phase falling-film cooling system This two-phase falling-film cooling system uses 3M™ Electronic Fluorinated Fluid HFE-7100 as the working fluid and operates as an atmospheric-pressure closed loop.
Heat Exchangers & Augmentation Investigation of thermal fins under natural convection - for thermosyphon and pulsating heat pipe thermal fin and typical fan-less heat sink This environmental chamber uses heaters and a proportional–integral–derivative controller to maintain the desired ambient temperature for natural-convection testing.
Electronics & AI Cooling Wind tunnel for air-cooler heat sink with Vapor chamber/heat pipe/pulsating heat pipe (and typical air-cooled heat sink) This wind tunnel facility evaluates the thermal performance of air-cooled electronic cooling devices, such as vapor chambers and heat sinks, under controlled airflow conditions.
Data-Center Thermal Management Experimental Data Center Facility (air-cooled, raise floor, up to 100 kW) Ten server racks arranged in two rows with cold and hot aisles
Boiling & Two-Phase Heat Transfer Two-Phase Cold Plate Cooling Test Platform This two-phase cooling test platform evaluates two-phase cold plates under controlled operating conditions.
Boiling & Two-Phase Heat Transfer Flooded evaporator for testing shell side boiling, applicable for various refrigerants This closed-loop external-tube pool-boiling system consists of two cylindrical stainless-steel vessels serving as the evaporator and condenser.
Boiling & Two-Phase Heat Transfer Experiment Setup for boiler surface Different surface enhancement
Boiling & Two-Phase Heat Transfer Two-phase cold plate test facility, up to 3 kW for various working fluids The cold plate setup supports up to 3 kW of power
HVAC&R, Heat Pumps & Dehumidification Vapor Compression Refrigeration Cooling System (VCRC) Two phase Cold Plate This vapor-compression refrigeration cooling system uses R-134a and consists of a compressor, condenser, refrigerant flow meter, electronic expansion valve, and two-phase cold plate.
Boiling & Two-Phase Heat Transfer Single Tube Boiling Test System for Refrigerant–Lubricant Mixtures This external-tube refrigerant test system evaluates refrigerant boiling performance, lubricant–refrigerant mixtures, and enhanced heat transfer surfaces.
HVAC&R, Heat Pumps & Dehumidification Liquid desiccant air-conditioning system This liquid-desiccant air-conditioning system integrates a vapor-compression heat pump with liquid-desiccant dehumidification using CreCOPlus® 5100C ionic liquid and R-407C refrigerant.
Electronics & AI Cooling MAKEAR Carvera Desktop CNC Machine Automated equipment that uses software to control the machine and tool positions for the production of high-precision parts; features four-axis machining and laser capabilities.
Electronics & AI Cooling Fabrication and microscopy equipment 3D Printer | Microscope
