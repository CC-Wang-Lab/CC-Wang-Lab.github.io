\begin{note}
This vapor-compression refrigeration cooling system uses R-134a and consists of a compressor, condenser, refrigerant flow meter, electronic expansion valve, and two-phase cold plate. Refrigerant temperature, pressure, flow rate, and cold-plate temperature are measured throughout the system. The experimental variables include compressor speed, heat load, and expansion-valve opening. The measured data are used to evaluate cooling capacity, heat transfer rate, thermal resistance, refrigerant enthalpy, vapor quality, and coefficient of performance.
\end{note}
