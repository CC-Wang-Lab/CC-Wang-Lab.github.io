\begin{level}{facility-airflow-diagram}
- Ten server racks arranged in two rows with cold and hot aisles
- Facility dimensions: 6.0 m × 3.1 m × 2.3 m
- Cold aisle containment with a 50% perforated-floor opening ratio
- Rack heat loads: 40 kW for existing racks and 12 kW for new racks
- Computer room air handler airflow rates: 2.4 and 3.4 m³/s
- Temperature and airflow measured using 175 thermocouples and 15 anemometers
- Server simulators include 2U and 4U chassis with heating modules rated up to 400 W each
- Performance evaluated using power usage effectiveness, rack cooling index, and supply heat index
- Rack inlet temperatures assessed using the recommended range of 18–27°C and allowable range of 15–32°C
\end{level}
