- **“Project enquiries”** removes the consultancy/sales tone of “Discuss an R&D project” while retaining the purpose of the button.
- I would drop **“chip to the plant room”** and **“whole-plant energy systems”**: both are good slogans, but the latter especially suggests a scope not established by the information given.
- The revised Projects subtitle also covers **computational work**; “measuring and building” makes the laboratory sound more exclusively experimental than it is.
- **“Laboratory head”** is preferable to “Principal investigator”: PI can denote a specific formal role on a funded project, whereas the stated fact is simply that Prof. Wang leads the laboratory.
- I would use **“HVAC and refrigeration”** rather than “HVAC&R” in prominent public-facing prose; it is clearer without becoming less technical.
